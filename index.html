Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does a planning signal mean a customer is ready to hire?

No. Planning records are public signals and do not confirm that a property owner wants to hire a contractor.

Are customer contact details shown publicly?

No. Personal contact details should remain private and only be shared through an approved, consent-based and secure process.

Can every contractor buy a lead?

No. Access is intended for approved contractor accounts and eligible leads. Payment and verification controls must be active before transactions launch.

Which areas does BuildLead cover?

The platform focuses on London. Coverage depends on the sources and project information currently available.

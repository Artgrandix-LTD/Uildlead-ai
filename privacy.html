Privacy Notice

Pre-launch notice: This is a starter template, not a completed legal privacy notice. Before collecting enquiries or publishing live property listings, the agency must add and verify its legal operator details, contact email and address, lawful bases, retention periods, processors, data-sharing arrangements and complaint route. Obtain appropriate UK data-protection advice.

Who operates this website?

This website is branded HUSLY ESTATES. The legal operator and registered business details must be clearly identified here before the site accepts personal information. HUSLY ESTATES should not be described as a registered company until its name and incorporation have been confirmed.

Information we may collect

When the enquiry service is activated, it may collect contact details and information about property searches, viewings, offers, lettings or property instructions. Only information necessary for the stated purpose should be requested.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange viewings, provide property services and meet applicable legal obligations. Any marketing use or sharing with third parties must have an appropriate lawful basis and be explained clearly.

Sharing and retention

Before launch, identify the service providers and other recipients who may process information, document any international transfers, and set retention periods appropriate to each purpose.

Your rights and contact

Publish a working privacy contact route and explain how people can exercise their UK GDPR rights or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.
